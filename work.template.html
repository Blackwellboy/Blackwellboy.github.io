Selected research studies

Controlled studies where the result mattered more than whether it agreed with the starting hypothesis.

Reasoning behaviour

Thinking-gate study

Thinking was not controlled by a flag or prompt length alone. A dense ten-rule persona block fired 3/40, while a much longer full agent prompt fired 24/40. Summarization fired 0/105; the same grid on Qwen 3.6 35B-A3B fired 400/400. The useful result is a prompt × task gate, not a simple dose-response story.

Conditions & reproducibility

400 grid turns, 10 conditions by 4 task types, n=10 per cell, 450 turns logged including parser and criteria phases, 0 HTTP errors, single-turn. Laguna S 2.1 NVFP4 rev 0761412 under vLLM 0.25.1 on one DGX Spark GB10. These are within-run cells on this build and do not generalise across quants or runtimes.

Benchmark replication

HumanEval+ replication

A published “thinking-on wins” result had the two arms running different sampling settings. With sampling matched, the gain disappeared and the sign reversed: ON 89.84, OFF 90.85. Thinking-on was steadier across seeds, but cost roughly ten times the wall clock: 200.2 s mean per problem versus 18.5 s.

Conditions & reproducibility

984 requests, 164 problems by 2 arms by 3 seeds, arms interleaved, identical sampling both arms at temperature 0.7 / top_p 0.95 / top_k 20, max_tokens 12,288, 0 transport errors, evalplus 0.3.1. NVFP4 rev 0761412 under vLLM 0.25.1 on one GB10.

Multi-turn serving

Multi-turn reasoning-stripping

In the OpenAI-style client path tested, prior assistant reasoning was not resent. The template then rendered earlier assistant turns with empty think blocks and the model stopped firing its reasoning gate. Identical transcripts fired 10/10 with reasoning preserved and 0/10 stripped at both depths tested.

Conditions & reproducibility

Depth 10 and depth 20 conversations, identical transcripts both arms, max_tokens 4096. Measured on the 0xSero 3.25bpw EXL3 hybrid lane. This is a mechanism result for the tested client/template path, not a claim that every OpenAI-compatible client strips reasoning.

Hypothesis test

Identity-prefix study

This was run expecting to confirm a community hypothesis that long system prompts suppress thinking by displacing the trained identity. It failed the direct test: moving identity to the literal first line took firing from 5/40 to 0/40. Neutral and topical filler reopened the gate by similar amounts, pointing away from identity itself.

Conditions & reproducibility

n=40 per cell, arms interleaved within each run, two builds. NVFP4 bare 2/40 against identity 17/40, neutral filler 10/40, topical filler 11/40. The 3.25bpw hybrid bare 0/40 against 13/40, 14/40, 10/40. Qwen 3.6 35B-A3B null control fired 480/480.

Performance tuning

20-cell tuning sweep

Speculative depth beyond K=7 made this model slower at every concurrency level tested. The single-stream winner reached 26.2 tok/s median and 61.65 tok/s aggregate at concurrency 4. The grid’s best aggregate was a different cell: K=6 with max-num-seqs 8 at 62.26 tok/s.

Conditions & reproducibility

20 cells, K 5 to 9 by max-num-seqs 4 to 32, single run per cell. NVFP4 rev 0761412 under vLLM 0.25.1 on one GB10, Hermes bench v1 short subset. Every losing cell is published.

Production stability

12-hour production soak

3,096 HTTP-200 turns across 409 sessions, with zero crashes and zero restarts, inside a real agent pipeline. Thinking fired on only 3 turns despite being requested every turn; the later reasoning-stripping study explains that anomaly.

Conditions, correction & reproducibility

One 12-hour run, production profile K=7 and max-num-seqs=32, max_tokens 8192. 3,099 rows logged; 3,096 returned HTTP 200. Correction 2026-07-25: 409 sessions = 400 completed + 9 killed at the driver token cap; the three extra lines are integrity probes and all returned HTTP 200.
