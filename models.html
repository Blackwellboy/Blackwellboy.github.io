How to read the status

Full deploymentDedicated bring-up work with a reproducible serving path and measured operating results.
Full research campaignMultiple controlled studies or long-duration runs with public evidence.
Targeted testA narrower question was measured directly; it is not presented as full model coverage.
ControlThe model exists to bound another claim, which is often more valuable than another benchmark row.

The full Minefield model map also contains upstream reports and contributor evidence. Those are deliberately not relabelled here as my first-hand work.

The model wall is curated from public evidence, not private campaign notes. A narrower public receipt gets a narrower status.

If something here does not check out, open an issue and it gets corrected in place, with a date.
