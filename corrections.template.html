Blackwellboy / Corrections

The result matters more than being right the first time.

A research site that only shows the wins is not very useful. This is where claims get retracted, narrowed or corrected, with the evidence that changed them left visible.

What changed

Retracted causal interpretation

Prompt dose did not explain reasoning depth

The original gate study measured median thinking depth of 3,536 tokens on a bare prompt and 282 under the heaviest agent prompt, and I interpreted that as prompt dose shortening reasoning. A dedicated 200-turn interleaved grid found depth among fired turns statistically flat, with all pairwise p-values at or above 0.13. The medians were real; the causal reading was not.

Corrected in place in c7-depth-collapse. The revised explanation is task composition plus truncation at the tool-call boundary.

Hypothesis refuted

The identity-prefix theory failed its direct test

I ran the identity-prefix study expecting to confirm a community hypothesis. Moving the identity to the literal first line made the measured effect worse, not better. The losing cells stayed in the published result.

See the identity-prefix study.

Claim narrowed

A larger external sample bounded my Laguna claim

I had described the binary thinking gate too broadly. Apollo-mg ran the same model on a different quant and runtime at n=492 and saw no gate closure, so the claim is now scoped to the builds and serving paths actually measured here.

Negative evidence kept

Losing, blocked and not-testable results stay visible

All 20 tuning-sweep cells are published, including the 19 that did not win. The Minefield mining area currently holds {{OPEN_QUESTIONS}} write-ups for candidates that did not reproduce, could not be tested, or remain specification-only. Several had confirm/refute criteria written before the run.

Propagation

Corrections have to reach every surface carrying the claim

When a reader caught a reasoning-field name being described as a property of the model rather than the serving stack, the correction went into the lab repo, the registry entry and the upstream thread. The Minefield integrity layer now checks for superseded phrasing across public surfaces.

Live repository facts are generated from source. Fixed-run measurements keep the exact conditions that produced them.

If something here does not check out, open an issue and it gets corrected in place, with a date.
