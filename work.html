Research studies

Six studies where the interesting part was what the stack actually did, not whether it matched the hypothesis going in.

Reasoning behaviour

Thinking-gate study

On this Laguna build, thinking was not controlled by a flag or prompt length alone. A dense ten-rule persona block fired 3/40, while a much longer full agent prompt fired 24/40. Summarization fired 0/105; the same grid on Qwen 3.6 35B-A3B fired 400/400. The useful result is a prompt × task gate, not a simple dose-response story.

Conditions & reproducibility

400 grid turns, 10 conditions by 4 task types, n=10 per cell, 450 turns logged including parser and criteria phases, 0 HTTP errors, single-turn. Laguna S 2.1 NVFP4 rev 0761412 under vLLM 0.25.1 on one DGX Spark GB10. These are within-run n=40 cells on this build and do not generalise across quants or runtimes: at n=492 on llama.cpp with a Q2_K_XL build, apollo-mg measured firing at 445/492 under a comparable apparatus.

Benchmark replication

HumanEval+ replication

A published “thinking-on wins” result had the two arms running different sampling settings. With sampling matched, the gain disappeared and the sign reversed: ON 89.84, OFF 90.85. Thinking-on was steadier across seeds, but cost roughly ten times the wall clock: 200.2 s mean per problem versus 18.5 s.

Conditions & reproducibility

984 requests, 164 problems by 2 arms by 3 seeds, arms interleaved in submission order, identical sampling both arms at temperature 0.7 / top_p 0.95 / top_k 20, max_tokens 12,288, 0 transport errors, evalplus 0.3.1 scoring. NVFP4 rev 0761412 under vLLM 0.25.1 on one GB10.

Multi-turn serving

Multi-turn reasoning-stripping

In the OpenAI-style client path tested, prior-turn reasoning was not sent back. The template then rendered earlier assistant turns with empty think blocks and the model quietly stopped thinking. Identical transcripts fired 10/10 with reasoning preserved and 0/10 stripped at both depths tested. The fix on that path is to resend reasoning on assistant history messages.

Conditions & reproducibility

Depth 10 and depth 20 conversations, identical transcripts both arms, max_tokens 4096. Measured on the 0xSero 3.25bpw EXL3 hybrid lane. Confirmation on the NVFP4 production lane is still open and remains listed as open in the study.

Hypothesis test

Identity-prefix study

This was run expecting to confirm a community hypothesis that long system prompts suppress thinking by displacing the trained identity. It failed the direct test: moving identity to the literal first line took firing from 5/40 to 0/40. Neutral and topical filler reopened the gate by similar amounts, pointing away from identity itself.

Conditions & reproducibility

n=40 per cell, arms interleaved within each run, two builds. NVFP4 bare 2/40 against identity 17/40, neutral filler 10/40, topical filler 11/40. The 3.25bpw hybrid bare 0/40 against 13/40, 14/40, 10/40. Qwen 3.6 35B-A3B null control fired 480/480.

Performance tuning

20-cell tuning sweep

In this grid, speculative depth beyond K=7 made the model slower at every concurrency level tested. The single-stream winner reached 26.2 tok/s median and 61.65 tok/s aggregate at concurrency 4. The grid’s best aggregate was a different cell: K=6 with max-num-seqs 8 at 62.26 tok/s.

Conditions & reproducibility

20 cells, K 5 to 9 by max-num-seqs 4 to 32, single run per cell. NVFP4 rev 0761412 under vLLM 0.25.1 on one GB10, Hermes bench v1 short subset, TTFT 322 ms at the single-stream winner. Every losing cell is published.

Production stability

12-hour production soak

3,096 HTTP-200 turns across 409 sessions, with zero crashes and zero restarts, inside a real agent pipeline. Thinking fired on only 3 turns despite being requested every turn; the later reasoning-stripping study explains that anomaly on the tested serving path.

Conditions, correction & reproducibility

One 12-hour run, production profile K=7 and max-num-seqs=32, max_tokens 8192, thinking requested on every turn. 3,099 rows logged; 3,096 returned HTTP 200. NVFP4 rev 0761412 under vLLM 0.25.1 on one GB10. Correction 2026-07-25: 409 sessions = 400 completed + 9 killed at the driver token cap; the three extra lines are kind: integrity_probe and all returned HTTP 200. Per-turn log at lab tip 4507826. Carried in TheTom’s offlabel guide as external validation.

Public deployment recipes

Complete bring-up recipes for large models on DGX Spark pairs. These are lane-specific measurements, not universal model-speed claims.

295B MoE / dual DGX Spark

Hy3 — NVFP4 + MTP

Tencent Hy3 across two DGX Sparks with TP=2, native MTP, FP8 KV cache and structured hy_v3 tool calling. Measured at 13.1 tok/s single-stream no-think, 9.4 tok/s thinking, and 36.9 tok/s six-way aggregate. MTP position-1 acceptance was 63.5%.

Accuracy slice N=100: GSM8K 98.7%; MMLU 90/100. Peak/node was about 120/121 GiB; cold start about 14 min.

Conditions & implementation notes

vLLM image eugr/spark-vllm, CUDA 13.0 default toolchain, marlin NVFP4-MoE path, FP8 KV, hy_v3 parsers patched in-container. The prebuilt image runs weight-only FP4 on sm_120; the repo documents the from-source sm_121a path as the speed lever. Full flags and accuracy methodology are in the linked README.

428B / dual DGX Spark

MiniMax-M3 — stock-driver recipe

Full 428B MiniMax-M3 on two DGX Sparks with the stock DGX Spark OS driver. The standing speculative lane measured 34.0 code / 41.1 structured / 25.5 prose tok/s. A drafterless fallback runs at roughly 23 tok/s across the tested shapes.

Deep run: 17.8 tok/s at 113K with TTFT around 147 s. The deployment finding was that the b12x attention lane crashed at first token on CUDA 13.0, while the KVarN lanes ran on the stock driver.

Conditions & implementation notes

W4A16 GPTQ weights + KVarN 4-bit-K/2-bit-V KV cache + EAGLE-3 speculative decoding, TP=2, all-Triton attention. Lane C acceptance 0.65 / 0.80 / 0.36 for code / structured / prose; KV pool 217,600 tok. Lane B prefill about 880 tok/s; KV pool 378,496 tok. Upstream stack pinned at a3refaat/spark-vllm-docker@f942437; image ghcr.io/blackwellboy/vllm-node-minimax-m3-b12x:cuda13.0-f942437.
