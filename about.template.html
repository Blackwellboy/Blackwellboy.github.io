Blackwellboy / About

Hands-on infrastructure, measured model serving.

I work on the physical side of modern AI infrastructure and use my own cluster to test what model-serving stacks actually do. The point is not the hardware list; it is having enough different environments to tell a model problem from a serving problem.

What I do

My day-to-day work is hands-on data-centre deployment: B200 and B300 class systems, cabling and installation, fibre splicing and fibre engineering, racking, commissioning, testing and maintenance.

On my own systems I bring that same deployment discipline to local AI: reproduce the claim, pin the build, measure the behaviour, keep the raw output, and publish the conditions that make the number meaningful.

The lab

The lab is a networked mix of DGX Sparks, multi-GPU workstation hardware and Apple silicon. Different architectures and runtimes are useful because disagreements are evidence: if the same weights behave differently across stacks, the serving path becomes part of the investigation.

How I publish

I separate fixed-run measurements from facts about a live repository. Measurements keep their exact runtime, hardware and test conditions. Repository counts are generated from the source tree. Negative results, blocked tests and corrections stay visible instead of being cleaned out of the story.
